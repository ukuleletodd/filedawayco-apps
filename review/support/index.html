Quick fixes

The download will not open

Download the ZIP to a computer or phone, then unzip it (on iPhone: Files app, tap the ZIP). PDFs open in any PDF viewer. Or skip the file and use the link in the Start Here PDF.

How do I put the app on my home screen?

Open the link from your Start Here PDF. Android/Chrome: menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen. iPhone: Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen. After that it opens like an app and works offline.

My data disappeared

Your data is saved only in the browser on that device. Clearing browser data, using private mode, or switching phones or browsers starts fresh. Use Save a backup file now and then, and Restore from backup on the new device.

My import file was not recognised

Use the CSV import template that came in your ZIP, or the template button inside the app. If the app says the file looks like a different kind, it will tell you which app it belongs to. Still stuck? Send me the column headings (not your data) and I will help.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No. There is no account, no login and no subscription. It is a one-time digital download.

Your data and privacy

Your data stays on your device. What you type or import is saved only in your browser on that device. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and I cannot see your numbers. The apps work offline once opened. The only network use is loading the app itself (and any link you choose to open).

Refunds for digital items

These are instant digital downloads, so they cannot be returned the way a physical item can. But if something does not work as described, or you bought the wrong item by mistake, message me and I will fix it, help you get it working, or make it right, including a refund where that is fair.

On Etsy, purchases are also covered by Etsy's own case system and Purchase Protection program; see Etsy Buyer Policy. If you are in the EU or UK you may have a legal right to withdraw from a digital purchase in some situations; that right is not reduced by anything on this page.

Rental Property Books

Open the app

Does it do my taxes?

No. It keeps your records and sorts them onto Schedule E lines for each rental, so your preparer has clean numbers. Your preparer decides what goes on your return. General information only, not tax, legal or financial advice.

Can I bring in my bank or Airbnb file?

Yes: CSV files from your bank or card, Airbnb, Vrbo, PayPal or Stripe. You see a preview, tick the rows to keep, and can undo the import. If a file is not recognised, use the matching CSV template from your download, or send me the column headings (not your data).

Does it work out depreciation?

No. Improvements, mortgage principal and deposits are listed separately so your preparer can work out depreciation and the rest.

Where are my receipt photos kept?

Only on your device, shrunk to save space. They are included when you tap Save a backup file, so keep that file somewhere safe.

Which mileage rate does it use?

The IRS standard rate for each trip date: 72.5 cents a mile to June 30, 2026 and 76 cents from July 1, 2026. For other years you can type the rate in More.
